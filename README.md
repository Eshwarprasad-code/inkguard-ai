## 📌 Project Overview

**InkGuard AI** is an intelligent brand-governance engine developed for the **Inkloom AI Hackathon (Round 2: The Build)**. 

Modern organizations produce hundreds of assets weekly across distributed employees, external agencies, and generative AI tools. While generative AI accelerates production, it introduces critical brand vulnerabilities: **unsupported performance claims, hallucinated product specs, off-brand tone, inaccessible color contrasts, and logo degradation.**

Rather than acting as a blunt gatekeeper, **InkGuard AI** serves as an **active, explainable co-pilot and verification runway**. It ingests living brand rules and verified company fact sheets, analyzes text and promotional banners concurrently, flags issues with exact line-and-pixel citations, and generates **three calibrated intent-preserving rewrite alternatives** (*Conservative*, *Balanced*, *Creative*) with role-based sign-offs and an immutable compliance audit trail.

## 🌟 Core Features & Modules

### 1. Dynamic Brand Knowledge Hub
* Centralized repository of brand guidelines, tone rules, and visual tokens.
* Pre-loaded presets: **Inkloom AI (Official Brand Kit)** and **TechNova Cloud**.
* Interactive modal to view, edit, or add verified corporate facts, custom banned terms, and visual design tokens.

### 2. Factual Claim Grounding Engine
* Cross-references numerical metrics, SLAs, and performance claims against an active **Approved Corporate Fact Base**.
* Eliminates AI hallucinations and flags ungrounded commercial superlatives.

### 3. Multimodal Visual & WCAG 2.1 Contrast Inspector
* Mathematical implementation of W3C WCAG 2.1 relative luminance and contrast ratio formulas:
  $$Contrast = \frac{L_1 + 0.05}{L_2 + 0.05}$$
* Automatically detects contrast failures (< 4.5:1 on text overlays) and logo safe-zone crowding (< 24px).

### 4. Intent-Preserving Reformulation Engine
* Unlike linters that reject content, InkGuard generates **three calibrated rewrite options**:
  * **Conservative:** Zero-risk, strict legal framing.
  * **Balanced (Recommended):** High promotional energy grounded in verified facts.
  * **Creative:** Social-first phrasing preserving author hook without policy breach.

### 5. Multi-Stakeholder Collaborative Review & Audit Trail
* Role-based workflow switcher: **✍️ Creator**, **🛡️ Brand Manager**, **⚖️ Legal Counsel**.
* In-line exemption requests with creator rationale.
* Cryptographic Compliance Certificate with verifiable SHA-256 hash and immutable event log.

### 6. The Guideline Ambiguity Radar
* Reverse-governance analytics dashboard tracking creator dispute rates and reviewer deliberation times.
* Surfaces confusing or contradictory corporate guidelines (e.g. *Rule §2.3: 46% Dispute Rate*) and provides actionable revision recommendations.
